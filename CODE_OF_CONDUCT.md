# Community Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are dedicated to building an open, welcoming, diverse, and harassment-free community for everyone participating in the Wordle AI project, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or personal characteristics.

## Expected Standards

Community members and contributors are expected to:
- Practice empathy, kindness, and patience in discussions and code reviews.
- Focus on constructive collaboration and respectful technical discourse.
- Welcome newcomers and offer constructive guidance when reviewing contributions.
- Respect differing perspectives, viewpoints, and skill levels.
- Prioritize the collective goals and ethical guidelines of the open source project.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Unacceptable behaviors include, but are not limited to:
- Harassing, derogatory, or discriminatory language, comments, or personal attacks.
- Unwelcome sexual attention, advances, or inappropriate imagery.
- Sustained disruption of issues, discussions, or community spaces.
- Publishing private information without explicit prior consent.
- Any conduct that reasonably causes harm or distress to community participants.

## Reporting & Enforcement

If you witness or experience unacceptable behavior, please contact the project maintainers directly at `community@wordle-ai.dev` or open a private issue with project leads. All reports will be handled confidentially and investigated promptly.

Maintainers maintain the right and responsibility to edit comments, remove contributions, or restrict access to any contributor engaging in behavior inconsistent with this code of conduct.
